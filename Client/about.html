The kitchen

Angithi is a small kitchen in Rajkot cooking over charcoal. Dal simmers overnight, kebabs go on the sigdi, and rotis come to the table too hot to hold.

Everything cooks over charcoal or in the clay oven. The menu is short and changes with what the market has that week.
